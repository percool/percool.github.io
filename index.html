Research

My research focuses on computer vision, including dynamic world understanding, reconstruction, and prediction. Specifically, I aim to build visual intelligence through spatio-temporal generation. My recent work includes uncertainty-aware dynamic Gaussian Splatting for 4D reconstruction and 3D tracking, feedforward scene reconstruction, 4D skeleton generation from meshes or videos via flow matching, and geometry-grounded world models.

Research Projects

  • Video Generation & World Models: Building geometry-grounded world models and video diffusion models that generate spatially consistent, long-horizon futures and arbitrary novel views of dynamic scenes, such as autonomous-driving environments.
  • 3D/4D Reconstruction & Novel View Synthesis: Recovering dynamic scenes and their structure from monocular or multi-view video with uncertainty-aware dynamic Gaussian Splatting and feedforward reconstruction models, enabling high-quality rendering at extreme viewpoints.
  • Perception for Robotic Grasping: Fusing cameras with fingertip pretouch sensors for active perception, transparent object reconstruction, and material and structure mapping before contact.
